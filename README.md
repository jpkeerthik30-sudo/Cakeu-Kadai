## THE CAKEU KADAI — HOME BAKER

**The Cakeu Kadai** is a modern, responsive bakery website created for a home-based cake and dessert business in **Keelaputhur, Usilampatti, Madurai**. The website provides a warm, visually appealing digital storefront where customers can explore homemade cakes, desserts, brownies, cupcakes, custom cakes, and gifting options.

The website emphasizes the bakery's **fresh, homemade, made-to-order approach**, highlighting that products are prepared in small batches using premium ingredients and without preservatives or dark artificial colours. Customers can browse the menu, check prices, view the gallery, read reviews, and place orders through WhatsApp.

### Key Features

* Modern and responsive bakery website
* Mobile, tablet, and desktop-friendly design
* Attractive hero section highlighting freshly made products
* Online menu with cakes, desserts, brownies, cupcakes, custom cakes, and gifting options
* Product descriptions and starting prices
* Featured best-selling and signature products
* Custom cake and theme cake enquiry functionality
* Product gallery showcasing the bakery's creations
* Customer reviews and testimonials
* Step-by-step ordering process
* WhatsApp-based ordering and enquiry system
* Local pickup and delivery information
* Bakery location and Google Maps integration
* Instagram integration
* FAQ and customer support sections
* Pre-booking information and baking schedule
* Clear presentation of ingredients and homemade preparation
* Responsive navigation and user-friendly interface

### Products Showcased

The website features products such as **Chocolate Fudge Cake, Classic Tiramisu, Berry Bliss Cake, Triple Chocolate Brownies, Gulab Jamun Cake, Red Velvet Cake, cupcakes, jar desserts, theme cakes, number cakes, photo cakes, gift boxes, and hampers**.

### Ordering System

The website provides a simple three-step ordering experience. Customers can select a cake or provide a reference design, share details such as flavour, size, date, time and message, confirm the order with an advance payment, and choose between local delivery or pickup. The business operates on a **pre-booking basis**, with orders generally booked in advance.

### Project Objective

The main objective of **The Cakeu Kadai** website is to establish a professional online presence for a local home bakery while making it easy for customers to **discover products, explore the menu, view previous creations, understand the ordering process, and contact the baker directly**.

The website effectively works as a **digital bakery storefront and ordering platform**, combining product presentation, brand storytelling, customer reviews, location information, and direct WhatsApp communication in one place.

### Overall

The project combines **modern web design, responsive development, product showcasing, visual storytelling, and direct customer communication** to create a friendly and premium online experience for a home bakery. Its design reflects the handmade and personal nature of the business while keeping the ordering process simple and accessible.
